      Reading the evidence

      A line is a research thread, not a chain of cause and effect.
      A station joined by a stem belongs to more than one thread.

      Station positions show date and thread membership, taken from each milestone's tagged research areas. They do not show influence. Influence appears only in a station's study card and in the lineage tracer, and only where a source documents it: the later work cites, extends or uses the earlier one. Links that are indirect, a matter of timing, or a later interpretation are labelled as such.

      Dates, authors, institutions and relationships come from primary sources: arXiv, conference proceedings, journals and official lab publications. Benchmark figures from product announcements are marked as reported by the vendor. The narrative and study notes are written from that research rather than quoted from it. Coverage runs to September 2026.

      Reading the scenes and experiments

      Every scene and experiment carries a label that says what kind of numbers it uses.

      From the data Dates, sizes and figures taken from the milestones on this page.
      Illustrative values Invented numbers that show how an idea works. They are not research results.
      Formula from the paper The formula and constants come from the paper; the picture around them is simplified.
      Exact arithmetic A real calculation on inputs you choose.
      Real computation A real search over this page’s own milestones.
      Scripted, with real lookups Reasoning steps written in advance; the tool calls really run.

      The experiments are teaching models, not reproductions of the original systems. Each one says in its technical detail what it simplifies.
